Hamster Secrets & Care Guide

Interactive facts every hamster lover should know!

🌻 How Big Do Cheek Pouches Actually Stretch?

Hamster cheek pouches are completely dry sacs without salivary glands! They extend all the way back to their hips, allowing them to carry up to half their own body weight in seeds and bedding back to their burrows!

🏃 How Far Do Hamsters Run Each Night?

In the wild or inside a safe wheel, a single dwarf or Syrian hamster can run up to 5 to 8 miles (8–13 km) in a single night! That is equivalent to a human running several full marathons back to back!

🛁 Why Should You Never Bathe a Hamster in Water?

Water washes away necessary natural protective oils from their fur coat and causes rapid dangerous hypothermia. Hamsters clean themselves naturally using specialized sand baths filled with dust-free reptile-safe sand!
